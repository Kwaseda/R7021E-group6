- In the narrow world the robot drives through a door of 0.40 m (inflation 0.105) or 0.30 m
  (inflation 0.09). The padding then closes the door behind it, and the run ends with most of
  the world unseen. A 0.40 m door leaves 0.11 m each side of the robot. Treat passages under
  0.50 m as a risk.
