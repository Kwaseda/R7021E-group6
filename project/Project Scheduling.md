# Project Scheduling

## Presentation Scheduling

We are open to evaluating your work and scheduling your presentation any day between the 20th and 30th of October. Because this duration overlaps with the examination period, each group may propose a few time slots. I will check the availability of the instruction team and schedule your presentations.  Please propose your time slots by 19th October.

Each group will get a 45-minute slot to present: 20 minutes for the presentation, 20 minutes for questions and discussion, and 5 minutes for comments from the review panel.

We expect the group report to be turned in at least one day before your presentation.
