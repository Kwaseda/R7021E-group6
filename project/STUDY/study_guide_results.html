What the scenario 2 table says

All runs above use the 2 cm safety margin (LOG D9). Predictions from Theory Guide §12 with the margin added: one disc needs 0.579 m, three discs need 0.300 m.

  • One disc rejects every gap of 0.50 m or less. At 0.50 m the real plank has 0.15 m to spare on each side, and the controller still refuses, because its model of the body does not fit. That's the cost of a conservative footprint, and the headline of research question 1.
  • Three discs pass every gap down to 0.30 m, the gap the proposal named, with real clearance (gap − 0.20)/2. At 0.28 m they stop, which is the margin's price. Without the margin (bags/margin0/) three discs passed 0.28 m too, but the same footprint grazed the obstacle in s1a.
  • A rejected run is safe, not a crash. The robot stops where its keep-out rings touch the posts and never touches anything.
